## Reply wording
Applies to replies, PR bodies, and commit messages. Length is set elsewhere.
- Open with the answer or the result. Stop when the content ends.
- Write whole sentences with their articles and verbs. Spell out arrows and abbreviations.
- State the point directly, without first naming what it is not.
- Use the plain word: "use", "is", "because", "to".
- List as many items as there are, not three by habit.
- Separate thoughts with periods and commas. Use a colon only before a list or an example.
- Bold only a term the reader needs to find again.
- Name the file, the mechanism, or the number instead of a feeling or a metaphor.
- Before writing headings or product copy, call the Skill tool with `deslop-copy`.
